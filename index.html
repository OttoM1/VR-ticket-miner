How this works

VR’s website only lets you search one day at a time. This tool checks 14 days for you. Use 4h window to keep trips arriving in the four hours before your deadline (e.g. by 14:00 → between 10:01 and 14:00), or Any time for the cheapest trip that day regardless of arrival.

Station names
